# Surface brief: homepage concept 13, Atlas

Scope: the whole homepage, as a version of concept 12 with elevated craft. Mode: Persuade. Audience priority: reputation and research first (peers, partners, funders, press), with prospective students second. It keeps every section, the section order, all approved copy, the nav, the live NGN feeds and the globe journeys. Constraints are in PRODUCT.md. The memorable moment is the network drawn as an atlas.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage reads as an atlas of one university system. Every section is pinned to a place on the network, and the globe is the page's spine, not one module among many. It refuses the stacked marketing page where the global network is a single mid-page feature.

OWN-WORLD:
- Grounds: map-paper white #FFFFFF and #FAFAFA, with ink #111111. Hairline graticule rules (1px #E5E5E5) act as the grid.
- Type: FF Real Head Light for display at atlas-plate scale, with tabular numerals for coordinates and figures.
- Place tags appear only where a source states a place: the globe journey stops. Everything unsourced (hero features, news, research, closer, footer) uses the network form, "14 campuses · U.S., U.K., Canada". News spotlights carry their real story date instead of a place.
- Color: Northeastern Red #C8102E only for the active place marker and route line (well under 25%).
- Scene: research officers, partners and press reading at desks in daylight, which forces the light ground. The hero film is the one dark plate.

STORY: The visitor sees one institution doing work in many places: research and partnerships first, then the paths people take, then the news by place, and then a clear way onward.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The full-bleed hero film is framed by an atlas header strip. The headline sits lower-left in FF Real Head Light. The header strip carries the network form over the hero, said once. The feature lineup is a hairline index list (names only, red dot on the active item), and the primary button is visible without scrolling.

FORM: Atlas, number 3 on the ordered list of 7 surface structures, seed key 87d797dc. Signature interaction: the place locator travels with the visitor, naming the network over the hero, news and research, and the exact stop place (city only, no coordinates or journey titles) at the globe. The motion grammar is calm exponential ease-out.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
