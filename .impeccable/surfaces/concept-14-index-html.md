# Surface brief: homepage concept 14, Cinema refined

Scope: the whole homepage, as a version of concept 12 with elevated craft. Mode: Persuade. Audience priority: reputation and research first, with prospective students second. It keeps every section, the section order, all approved copy, the nav, the live NGN feeds and the globe journeys. Constraints are in PRODUCT.md. The memorable moment is the opening title sequence.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Concept 12's streaming world, made at the level of a festival title sequence: one continuous dark screening in which research leads the lineup. It refuses the generic streaming clone of identical shelves and hover-zoom cards.

OWN-WORLD:
- Ground: projection black #0B0B0E and #000, with ink #FAFAFA and the greys #A3A3A3 and #737373 for secondary text.
- Type: FF Real Head Light (200–300) for display at cinematic scale, with tracking no tighter than -0.03em. Medium for small labels.
- Imagery: restrained grain and a vignette on photography.
- Color: Northeastern Red #C8102E only on the active progress line and the Giving hover.
- Scene: evening browsing in low light on laptops and phones.

STORY: The visitor is shown a screening of the university: research first, then the network and how people move through it, then the stories, and then a clear close.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed film with the title at up to 6rem Light, lower-left. The synopsis runs to at most 65 characters per line, with one button. The lineup is a chapter rail of hairline progress lines along the bottom edge. The hero lineup order leads with Research.

FORM: Cinema refined, number 2 on the ordered list of 7 surface structures, seed key 87d797dc. Signature interaction: the chapter change is a blur dissolve between films, with the title set line by line. One authored motion moment, with no scattered effects.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
