# Surface brief: homepage concept 15, Wayfinding

Scope: the whole homepage, as a version of concept 12 with elevated craft. Mode: Persuade. Audience priority: reputation and research first, with prospective students second. It keeps every section, the section order, all approved copy, the nav, the live NGN feeds and the globe journeys. Constraints are in PRODUCT.md. The memorable moment is the directory.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is the university's directory sign: big, plain, high-contrast type that tells each visitor where to go next, with the films, stories and globe as the evidence beside each sign. It refuses the image-wall homepage where navigation hides in a hamburger.

OWN-WORLD:
- Panels: pure black #000000 and white #FFFFFF alternate as sign faces on a strict modular panel grid.
- Type: FF Real Head Medium and Bold for directional labels at large size, Light for running text, with tabular numerals.
- Arrows: authored one-stroke SVG.
- Color: Northeastern Red #C8102E only as the "you are here" marker and active direction (well under 25%).
- Scene: visitors scanning quickly on any device, with the goal of finding their door.

STORY: Within seconds the visitor finds their path (Research, Global Network, Admissions, Entrepreneurship, AI, Giving), sees the evidence behind each one, and moves on.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The hero film is kept, with the feature lineup recast as a directory panel: large type destinations, each with an arrow. The active destination carries the red marker. The headline and one button stay visible.

FORM: Wayfinding, number 5 on the ordered list of 7 surface structures, seed key 87d797dc. Signature interaction: pointing at a destination in the directory switches the film and headline to that feature, so the directory and the hero are one control. The motion grammar is short, decisive slides with exponential ease-out.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
