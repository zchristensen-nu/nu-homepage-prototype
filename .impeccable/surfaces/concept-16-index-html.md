# Surface brief: homepage concept 16, Conversation

Scope: the whole homepage, as a version of concept 12 with elevated craft (same rules as concepts 13–15). Mode: Persuade. Audience priority: reputation and research first, with prospective students second. It keeps every section, the section order, all approved copy, the nav, the live NGN feeds and the globe journeys. Constraints are in PRODUCT.md. The memorable moment is the composer.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage borrows the design language of a conversational AI product (the user named ChatGPT's) and applies it to a university: a quiet, near-chromeless interface where content arrives as calm, streamed responses and the visitor is invited to ask. It refuses the loud marketing homepage. It is inspired by that language, with no OpenAI marks, names or imitation of its product UI as a working chat.

OWN-WORLD:
- Grounds: white #FFFFFF with #F5F5F5 surfaces and ink #111111.
- Shapes: soft 16–28px radii on surfaces and pills, and a single rounded composer pill with a circular send control.
- Type: FF Real Head Regular and Light at modest, readable sizes; generous line height; at most 680px measure for running text.
- Lines and shadows: hairline #E5E5E5 dividers and almost no shadows.
- Color: Northeastern Red #C8102E only for the send control's active state and active markers.
- Scene: a visitor at a laptop or phone in daylight who wants an answer fast.

STORY: The visitor meets a calm surface that answers the question "What is Northeastern?" through the existing sections, delivered as response blocks. They see research and the network, and they can always search (the composer) or move on.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- The hero film sits in a large rounded media card.
- The headline and synopsis appear as a response that streams in once, word by word, with no cursor gimmick.
- The feature lineup becomes suggestion chips.
- A composer pill using the existing "Search Northeastern" placeholder sits below and submits to the existing search.
- The primary button is visible.

FORM: Conversation, user-named direction (added after seed key 87d797dc). Signature interaction: choosing a suggestion chip streams that feature's headline and synopsis into the response block (once, calmly, instantly under reduced motion). The motion grammar is short fades and ease-out; text streaming is the one authored moment.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
