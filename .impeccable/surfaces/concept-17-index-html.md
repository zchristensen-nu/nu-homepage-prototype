# Surface brief: homepage concept 17, Annual report

Scope: the whole homepage, as a version of concept 12 with elevated craft (same rules as concepts 13–15). Mode: Persuade. Audience priority: reputation and research first, with prospective students second. It keeps every section, the section order, all approved copy, the nav, the live NGN feeds and the globe journeys. Constraints are in PRODUCT.md. The memorable moment is the report's opening spread.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is the university's annual report, a calm, authoritative publication where research and partnerships lead and every claim sits on a figure, plate or caption. It refuses the image-wall marketing page and the generic broadsheet-editorial look (no italic serif, no tracked mono labels).

OWN-WORLD:
- Report spreads: a white #FFFFFF and #FAFAFA page on a strict 12-column grid with wide margins.
- Type: FF Real Head Light for display at report-cover scale and Regular for body.
- Figures: set large in tabular numerals with a rule above, plus captions under every image.
- Color: Northeastern Red #C8102E for one chart or section accent per spread. Gold #A4804A at about 5% at most as a hairline on the report's cover rule.
- Scene: partners, funders and press reading carefully at a desk.

STORY: Research leads, then the network, then news and outcomes, and the page closes with ways to act.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A cover spread.
- The film runs as a plate occupying two thirds of the width.
- The headline and synopsis sit in the other third, on paper.
- The lineup becomes a contents list with page-style numerals only if they carry real order. Otherwise it's a plain contents list.
- One button.

FORM: Annual report, number 1 on the ordered list of 7 (not dealt in round one; the user requested it), seed key 87d797dc. Signature interaction: the figures and plates settle into their grid as each spread enters, once, with exponential ease-out. The stats are set statically: no count-up.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
