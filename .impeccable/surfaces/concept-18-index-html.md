# Surface brief: homepage concept 18, Lab notebook

Scope: the whole homepage, as a version of concept 12 with elevated craft (same rules as concepts 13–15). Mode: Persuade. Audience priority: reputation and research first, with prospective students second. It keeps every section, the section order, all approved copy, the nav, the live NGN feeds and the globe journeys. Constraints are in PRODUCT.md. The memorable moment is the instrument readout.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage reads as a research instrument and lab notebook: evidence presented the way researchers record it, with numbered figures, captions, measurement marks and readouts. Research is working evidence, not marketing. It refuses the glossy brochure, and mono-as-costume.

OWN-WORLD:
- Grounds: off-white #FAFAFA notebook ground with a faint #E5E5E5 measurement grid (the grid is structural, not decorative noise). Ink #111111.
- Type: FF Real Head with tabular numerals for every number, and small caption type on numbered figures ("Fig." only where a figure exists).
- Marks: hairline tick marks and scales on rules.
- Color: Northeastern Red #C8102E as the instrument's active trace and readout highlight, under 25%.
- Scene: researchers and partners scanning for substance.

STORY: The visitor sees the evidence first, research and the network as measured activity, and then the stories as field notes, with clear onward paths.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- The hero film sits in a captioned figure frame with tick-mark rulers on two edges.
- The headline and synopsis sit beside it as the notebook entry.
- The lineup is a row of instrument channels with a live progress trace.
- One button.

FORM: Lab notebook, number 4 on the ordered list of 7, seed key 87d797dc. Signature interaction: the hero lineup's progress is drawn as a live instrument trace (an SVG line advancing along a scale), and the globe journeys' progress uses the same trace language. Calm, no jitter.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
