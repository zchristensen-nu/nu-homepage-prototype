# Surface brief: homepage concept 19, Exhibition

Scope: the whole homepage, as a version of concept 12 with elevated craft (same rules as concepts 13–15). Mode: Persuade. Audience priority: reputation and research first, with prospective students second. It keeps every section, the section order, all approved copy, the nav, the live NGN feeds and the globe journeys. Constraints are in PRODUCT.md. The memorable moment is entering a room.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is an exhibition of the university: each section is a gallery room with large plates, wall text and a curated walk-through, where the work leads and the interface recedes. It refuses the dense feed homepage.

OWN-WORLD:
- Rooms: alternating gallery walls, warm-neutral white (#FAFAFA) rooms and one dark room (#111111) for the films and globe. These are wall colors, not cream paper.
- Plates: big, generously spaced, with object labels (title, then a short line) set beside plates the way museum wall labels sit.
- Type: FF Real Head Light for wall titles, Regular for labels.
- Color: Northeastern Red #C8102E only as the small marker on the current room and on active controls.
- Scene: an unhurried visitor with time to look; a large desktop screen or a phone held upright.

STORY: The visitor walks room by room (films, news, the network, research, the closing room), each with one clear thing to look at and a way onward.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- The hero film is the first room's single plate, inset with wide wall margins, not full-bleed.
- The headline is the wall title, with the synopsis as wall text beside it.
- The lineup is a room index.
- One button.

FORM: Exhibition, number 7 on the ordered list of 7, seed key 87d797dc. Signature interaction: crossing into each room, the wall color shifts and the room's plate lifts gently into place (once per room, exponential ease-out, nothing under reduced motion).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
