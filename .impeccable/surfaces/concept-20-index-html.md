# Surface brief: homepage concept 20, Passport

Scope: the whole homepage, as a version of concept 12 with elevated craft (same rules as concepts 13–15). Mode: Persuade. Audience priority: reputation and research first, with prospective students second. It keeps every section, the section order, all approved copy, the nav, the live NGN feeds and the globe journeys. Constraints are in PRODUCT.md. The memorable moment is the stamp landing.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One university system rendered as one passport: the visitor holds a single document that is stamped at every campus and journey. Moving across the network without transferring is the passport's whole idea. It refuses every university-homepage convention. This is the bold wildcard, a foreign form (catalog challenger: vernacular-ephemera-well-traveled-passport), fused with product truth.

OWN-WORLD:
- Cover and pages: a deep black cover (in place of burgundy, to stay in brand), with a gold-blocked crest-like rule using gold #A4804A at about 5% at most. Data pages carry fine guilloché-style security line patterns drawn in SVG in brand greys (#E5E5E5 and #D4D4D4) on white.
- Type: FF Real Head caps with tracking for page headers, and tabular numerals for page numbers and dates. No OCR or monospace face (the brand requires FF Real Head).
- Stamps: authored SVG in Northeastern Red #C8102E, landing at slight angles over a ruled page grid. Stamps mark only real places: the globe journey stops and the campus list.
- Scene: a curious visitor, often on a phone, who will remember "the passport homepage".

STORY: The visitor opens the cover (the hero) and turns pages: news, the network (where journeys stamp the pages), research and the closing page. They can always leave through the nav or the buttons.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- The hero film runs inside the cover's window.
- The headline and synopsis are set as the cover lines.
- The lineup is the passport's tabbed page edge.
- One button.
- The nav stays standard and usable.

FORM: Passport, bold wildcard (catalog challenger, user-requested bolder), seed key 87d797dc. Signature interaction: as each globe journey stop lands, its stamp presses onto the page once (one press with a slight settle, then still forever). Instant under reduced motion. Calm, with no camera changes.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
