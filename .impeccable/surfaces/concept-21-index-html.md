# Surface brief: homepage concept 21, Cinema Conversation

Scope: the whole homepage. The user asked to see concept 16's Conversation hero carried into concept 14's Cinema design treatment ("I really love the hero [of Conversation] and would be interested in seeing this type of concept on with the Cinema design treatment"). Built from concept 14 after its latest fixes. Mode: Persuade. Audience priority: reputation and research first. Same rules as before: every section, the section order, approved copy, nav, live NGN feeds and globe journeys all stay. The memorable moment is the composer on the projection screen.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Cinema refined (the dark projection world, the festival-title craft, and research leading the lineup), with a conversational hero. The film is the screen, the response streams onto it, and the visitor is invited to ask. It refuses both the generic streaming clone and a bright chat UI pasted onto a dark page.

OWN-WORLD:
- Everything from concept 14 below the hero, unchanged: the #0B0B0E/#000 ground, ink #FAFAFA, FF Real Head Light display, grain and vignette on imagery, hairlines.
- The Conversation hero vocabulary, translated to dark:
  - a response column (headline, synopsis, one button, source pills)
  - suggestion chips for the lineup
  - a rounded composer pill (the real site search, with its existing "Search Northeastern" placeholder) with a circular send control
  - the film as the large media plate
- Hero surfaces: pills and composer are translucent-dark or hairline-bordered, not white.
- Color: Northeastern Red #C8102E only on the send control's active state, the active chip dot and active progress.

STORY: The visitor sees the screening open with research first. The response streams in once, they can pick a chip or search, and then the programme continues as in concept 14.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- The film fills the stage in the Cinema way: full-bleed or matted, with grain and vignette.
- The response column sits over the film's darkened side: headline in FF Real Head Light at cinematic scale, streaming once on load and on chip choice; synopsis; one button; three source pills.
- The chips sit below with a pause/play control, and the composer is anchored at the bottom of the column.

FORM: Cinema Conversation, user-requested fusion of concept 14 (#2 of 7, seed key 87d797dc) and concept 16. Signature interaction:
- The response streams onto the film word by word once, and again on a chip choice; autoplay advances use a plain fade.
- The film change keeps concept 14's blur dissolve, which never dips to black.
- The composer persists into the nav bar on wide screens, fading in without moving the links.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
