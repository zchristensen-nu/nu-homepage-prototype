# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The www.northeastern.edu homepage is the university's front door for several audiences at once. When trade-offs come up, the homepage serves reputation and research audiences first: peer institutions, research partners, funders, government and industry collaborators, and press, who arrive to judge what Northeastern is and what it is doing. Prospective students and families, current students, alumni and faculty are served on the same page, but they come second when a choice has to be made.

## Product Purpose

Show, in one visit, what makes Northeastern a different kind of university, and send each visitor on to the part of the university they came for. Success is a visitor who leaves able to describe that difference and who found their next page (admissions, research, the global network, giving) without hunting.

## Positioning

Three things a neighboring university cannot truthfully copy:

- **Experiential learning at scale:** co-op, a century-old model of full-time paid work woven into the degree.
- **One global university system:** campuses across the U.S., Canada and the U.K. where students and researchers move between locations without transferring, plus N.U.in first-semester locations abroad.
- **Research tied to that network:** institutes that run across campuses with industry, government and community partners.

## Operating Context

- This is a high-fidelity redesign prototype, not the production site. It is reviewed by the university digital team and shared as GitHub Pages links (`concept-N/` folders).
- Story content is pulled live from Northeastern Global News (news.northeastern.edu REST API).
- The page is reviewed on desktop and on phones.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Each concept is one self-contained HTML file per folder. Concepts 10–12 are generated by `tools/build_c9.py`.
- Hero video and imagery are hot-linked from northeastern.edu, research.northeastern.edu, admissions and news.northeastern.edu.
- There's an interactive canvas globe (the "Where a Northeastern path can go" section): globe motion is locked, journeys between stops play automatically, and the user has asked for calm motion (it caused motion sickness).
- Navigation: Admissions, Experiential Learning, Research, Global Network, Entrepreneurship, AI (each a dropdown), plus search, More and a Giving button.
- Concept 12 is the saved baseline (git tag `concept-12-v1`). New versions are duplicates and must not change it.

## Brand Commitments

- Typeface: FF Real Head (Light 200 through Bold 700, served from northeastern.edu), with Lato as the fallback. The live site uses a light-weight aesthetic.
- Color:
  - Black `#000000` and white `#FFFFFF` are the primary pair. Digital greys are `#111111`, `#FAFAFA`, `#F5F5F5`, `#E5E5E5`, `#D4D4D4`, `#A3A3A3`, `#737373` and `#404040`.
  - Northeastern Red `#C8102E` is an accent only and stays under 25% of any design.
  - Gold `#A4804A` and `#C8A978` are prestige accents, at about 5% at most.
- Logo: the "Northeastern University" lockup from the live site header (`tools/nu-lockup.svg`), which collapses to the red N on scroll.
- Voice: plain and specific. No slogans that read as AI filler, no student names on the globe, and no kickers or eyebrows over headings.
- Globe journeys show only city and note per stop: no "Student" / "Research" kind labels and no journey titles. The user removed them on purpose ("we can get rid of 'Student' / 'Research'", and earlier asked for no AI-sounding titles). Illustrative journeys are disclosed in the copy record and handoffs, not labelled on the page.
- All copy on concept 12 is the approved copy (Notion: "Homepage concept 12: all copy").

## Evidence on Hand

- **Live NGN story feeds:** Latest, Entrepreneurship, Human-Centered AI, University News, Research.
- **Hero films:** five, one per feature, from university sites.
- **Globe journeys:** three are real, from NGN reporting (N.U.in Greece→Boston, Network Science Institute, Global Scholars). Eleven are illustrative and must not be presented as reported fact.
- **Research stats:** $296M in awards, 50+ centers and 510 patents are carried from earlier copy and are not yet verified.
- **No invented content:** do not invent testimonials, rankings or figures.

## Product Principles

- Show the network in motion: the difference is that people and research move across one system, and the page should make that visible, not just state it.
- Lead with substance for expert eyes: research, partnerships and evidence first, with admissions one clear path among several.
- Calm, confident motion: motion explains; it never makes a visitor queasy or waits on them.
- Every section has a way onward: no dead ends.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA at minimum: 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text and UI. Honor reduced-motion settings, keep the page keyboard-navigable, and keep autoplaying content pausable.
